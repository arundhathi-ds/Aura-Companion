# Product Overview: Aura Companion

Aura Companion is a premium, emotionally intelligent AI universe designed to help users reflect, create, and find quiet moments in their daily lives.

## 1. Value Proposition
In a world of noisy social media and loud productivity tools, Aura Companion offers a **quiet, immersive space**. It doesn't ask for your attention; it gives you a mirror for the emotions you already have.

## 2. Core Pillars
*   **Presence**: A floating AI Orb that provides a constant, non-judgmental "other" in the digital space.
*   **Action**: Transforming "feelings" into "doings" through the Dynamic Experience Engine.
*   **Memory**: Building a visual and text-based archive of growth over time.
*   **Aesthetics**: A "Cinematic Glassmorphism" UI that makes every interaction feel intentional and high-end.
